Why GOUSA exists

Every year, thousands of U.S.-trained international graduates face hard visa deadlines (OPT timelines, start-date uncertainty). Employers don’t miss these candidates because of skill — they miss them because decisions happen too late.

Timing beats talent

Great candidates get filtered out when start dates and visa windows aren’t visible early.

Noise hides signal

Recruiters waste cycles in feeds, job boards, and unranked pipelines — while top candidates disappear.

Anxiety has a cost

Students face visa pressure, loans, and uncertainty — GOUSA restores visibility at the moment it matters.

This is not a talent shortage problem. It’s a timing and prioritization problem.
